## Overview
Healthcare was developed as a team software-engineering project for older adults. The application brings health information, a medicine database, and monitoring-related features into one mobile interface.

## My contribution
I organized the team and contributed backend development, connecting application requirements with service logic and data management. The project involved coordinating frontend and backend work, defining interfaces, and integrating the application.
