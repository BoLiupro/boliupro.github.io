## Overview
Vehicles entering tunnels or dense urban areas can lose reliable GNSS signals. My final-year project at Fuzhou University investigated C-V2X-assisted positioning as a way to maintain localization in these environments.

## Method
The MATLAB simulation combines roadside-unit measurements, inertial navigation, and laser ranging. Weighted least squares and Kalman filtering support the estimation process.

## Evaluation
The thesis studies positioning error and its convergence as vehicles enter tunnels under different motion patterns. It also discusses GRU-assisted localization as a possible extension. The report includes the simulation assumptions, system equations, and experimental results.
