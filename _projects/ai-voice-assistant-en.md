## Overview
This prototype explores a conversational interface through physical hardware. The accompanying recording demonstrates the device and its interaction flow.

## Hardware exploration
The project materials compare Raspberry Pi and ESP32 development options, including network connectivity, microphones, speaker interfaces, and audio input/output. These investigations support the selection of a practical platform for a voice assistant.
