## Overview
AgentApp connects a cloud-side collective pattern builder with user-side agents that maintain personal context. Its goal is to use shared behavioral knowledge without losing the individual habits and intentions that make prediction personal.

## From semantic reasoning to agents
Earlier work, LLMApp, used behavioral compression and contextual interpretation for hierarchical prediction. AgentApp extends this research toward persistent memory, retrieval, and collaboration between specialized agents.

## System components
- **Collective Pattern Builder:** organizes population-level usage patterns into a retrievable behavioral knowledge base.
- **Personalized Pattern Profiler:** maintains long- and short-term memories of individual habits and intent.
- **App Usage Predictor:** combines retrieved knowledge with personal context to reason about the next application.
- **Adaptive Collaboration Reflector:** updates memory and collective knowledge while improving agent coordination.

## Results
The AgentApp manuscript reports improvements of over 7% across its evaluated metrics on two real-world datasets, with further studies of transferability and the reuse of collective knowledge. The related papers describe the evaluation settings.
