## Overview
This undergraduate project connects a wrist-worn heart-rate sensor to an Arduino-based walking-stick unit. It explores how sensing, wireless communication, and alerts can be integrated into an assistive-device prototype.

## System
The design uses two Arduino boards and Bluetooth modules to transfer sensor readings between the wristband and the cane. The project focuses on signal acquisition, communication between devices, and the resulting feedback flow.

## Prototype and scope
The video and report document an educational engineering prototype. The demonstration concerns sensor communication and alert behavior; it is not a clinically validated heart-attack detection system.
