## Overview
This technical research project examines uncertainty in positioning estimates derived from electronic reconnaissance observations. The central question is how errors in measurements and coordinate descriptions propagate into an estimated position, and how that uncertainty can be represented clearly in a technical evaluation.

## Study scope
The report considers direction-based and time-difference-based positioning at a conceptual level. It organizes the analysis around observation geometry, coordinate transformations, and different sources of measurement uncertainty. The cover illustration comes from the report's discussion of coordinate systems.

## Error analysis and evaluation
The study distinguishes the contributions of individual uncertainty sources from their combined effect. Mathematical analysis and simulation are used together to compare predicted error behavior with simulated outcomes and to examine the sensitivity of the resulting estimates.

Evaluation includes measures of geometric error amplification and the spatial spread of position estimates. Error curves and spatial visualizations help communicate where an estimate is more or less sensitive to the assumptions used in the model.

## Research output
The project materials bring together a technical report, coordinate-system illustrations, and simulation-based error studies. The work emphasizes making modeling assumptions explicit and interpreting estimation uncertainty, connecting mathematical modeling with the communication of simulation results.
