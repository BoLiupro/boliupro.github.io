## About Me

I am **Bo Liu**, a master's student in Software Engineering at the College of Computer Science and Electronic Engineering, Hunan University.

My research started from **graph learning and diffusion modeling for spatiotemporal mobility prediction**, and later expanded toward **LLM-based semantic reasoning for human behavior modeling**. I am currently interested in **LLM-based agentic AI operating over complex structured environments**, with a particular focus on spatiotemporal intelligence and mobile data mining.

More broadly, I am interested in how language-model agents can reason, retrieve knowledge, collaborate, use tools, and interact with structured physical or digital environments.

## Research Interests

- Agentic AI and Multi-Agent Systems
- Large Language Models
- Spatiotemporal Intelligence
- Mobile Data Mining
- Graph Learning and Diffusion Models
- Human Mobility and Mobile App Usage Modeling

## Selected Research Projects

- **LLMApp** — LLM-based hierarchical reasoning for mobile app usage prediction.
- **UniMob** — unified cross-city human mobility generation with LLM guidance and diffusion models.
- **MAEDE** — multi-granularity graph diffusion for private-car activity prediction.
- **RAG²-MP** — retrieval-augmented and gravity-aware mobility prediction.
